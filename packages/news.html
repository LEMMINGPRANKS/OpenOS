OpenOS 1.4.1 is out!

THE INTERNET APP. Names, not numbers. Real DNS, from scratch.

What's new in 1.4.1

  • The Internet app: browser + news + updates + comments + ideas, one window, five tabs (Tab key cycles them)
  • Real DNS: our own resolver over our own UDP stack -- type example.com in the address bar and it just loads. A real website, fetched by OpenOS
  • Comments: read them AND post them from inside OpenOS (HTTP POST, our own request builder)
  • Ideas tab: the roadmap lives on the server -- post your improvement ideas straight from the app
  • Shell power tools: dns example.com and fetch example.com/

Coming next

A search engine (posted by the BDFL in the Ideas tab), bookmarks, and more of the real web.
