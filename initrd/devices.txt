OpenOS device registers (the law, by Freddie, BDFL)

DR = drive register, IR = internal register, UR = unsupported register

DR1  main drive
DR2  SD card slot
DR3  USB slot
IR1  RAM
IR2  initramfs (this filesystem!)
UR1  unsupported-device trap: constantly watches for unsupported
     memory; when it fires, the kernel crashes ON PURPOSE so the
     bad thing can never sneak past.
